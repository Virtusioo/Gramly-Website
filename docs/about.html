About Gramly

Gramly was made by 9 passionate high-school students
who wanted to make learning fun, interactive, and
accessible for everyone. Explore our card-based system
featuring interactive, puzzle, and mythical cards!

Fun Facts

All 9 team members contributed ideas
for design, graphics, and content.

Most team members were beginners
in programming at the start.

The project started as a
high-school research project.
