About Gramly

Gramly was created by 7 passionate high-school students
who wanted to make baking more accurate, consistent,
and accessible. It helps users create and manage recipes
using weight-based measurements, ingredient ratios,
and baking calculations.

Fun Facts

Gramly started as a high-school research project
focused on improving measurement accuracy in baking.

Gramly uses weight-based measurements to reduce
inconsistencies caused by differences in ingredient
density, packing, and measuring techniques.

Gramly supports Baker's Percentage, making it easier
to understand ingredient ratios and scale recipes
accurately.
