For Students

Gramly makes learning visual, fun,
and easier to understand. Here’s how to get started:

How to Use Gramly

1. Open a Subject:
Choose a lesson or topic you want to explore.
Each subject includes sets of learning cards.

2. Explore the Cards:
• Interactive Cards let you tap, move, or reveal information.
• Puzzle Cards strengthen logic and comprehension.
• Mythical Cards add fun challenges and collectible rewards.

3. Earn Points & Rewards:
Completing cards gives you points, badges,
or unlocking new sets.

4. Review Anytime:
You can replay cards to practice concepts
or to improve your score.

For Teachers

Gramly can be used as a classroom tool
or an independent learning companion.

Classroom Use

1. Select a Topic:
Pick a card set that matches your lesson.
You can assign specific card groups depending
on the student’s level.

2. Use Cards as Activities:
Interactive and puzzle cards work well as warm-ups,
comprehension exercises, or collaborative tasks.

3. Encourage Exploration:
Students can freely explore extra cards
to challenge themselves or review concepts.
