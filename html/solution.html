Our Solution

To address the challenges of illiteracy, the project is a learning platform
that emphasizes image-based instruction, visual imagery, and engaging activities.
By transforming traditional text-heavy lessons into interactive visual content,
the platform aims to make learning easier to understand, easier to retain,
increase enjoyability, and adaptable to all users.

Our Beneficiaries

Gramly is an application focused on helping individuals who suffer from dyslexia
and other reading-related challenges. The target users include people of all ages who
were born as slow readers, poor spellers, or struggling writers, many of whom may have
never received the proper support in traditional educational settings.
